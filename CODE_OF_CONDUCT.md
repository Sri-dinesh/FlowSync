# Community guidelines

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss the code and evidence,
give constructive feedback, and allow people to correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable. Respect dataset/model
licenses and credit others' work. Keep research claims tied to reproducible
evidence; do not fabricate outcomes or conceal unfavorable results.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and
other project-managed spaces. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content
or restrict participation to protect the community.

For a sensitive conduct concern, request a private contact channel from the
maintainer without posting identifying details publicly. Security reports
should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
