## 2. In-Depth Audit of the Four Secondary Result Files

### 2.1. File 1: `results/final/cv_validation_metrics.json`

- **File Metadata**: 1,625 bytes, JSON format.
- **Associated Tables/Figures**: `results/final/table_cv_perception_validation.tex` (Table VII in draft), `IEEE-conference-template-062824/fig6_cv_correlation.png`.
- **Claimed Content**:
  - Reports 1,000 annotated video frames evaluated across four environmental regimes:
    * Low-Density Daylight (250 frames): Precision 0.943, Recall 0.928, Count MAE 0.32, Lane Acc 96.8%, Mean Uncertainty 0.137.
    * Dense Commute Peak (300 frames): Precision 0.893, Recall 0.864, Count MAE 0.87, Lane Acc 92.4%, Mean Uncertainty 0.389.
    * Heavy Vehicle Occlusion (200 frames): Precision 0.843, Recall 0.781, Count MAE 1.45, Lane Acc 86.5%, Mean Uncertainty 0.626.
    * Adverse Rain & Low-Light (250 frames): Precision 0.813, Recall 0.750, Count MAE 1.72, Lane Acc 84.1%, Mean Uncertainty 0.713.
  - Overall Precision 0.873, Recall 0.831, Count MAE 1.09, Lane Acc 89.9%, Pearson $r = 0.928$ between calibrated uncertainty and perception error.
- **True Provenance & Origin**:
  - Generated by an early legacy script (`research/legacy/validate_perception_quality.py`).
  - That script was audited and disabled with the following explicit runtime notice:
    ```python
    def run_cv_validation_evaluation(*args, **kwargs):
        raise RuntimeError(
            "Disabled: this generator fabricated perception metrics without annotations. "
            "Use research.cv.evaluate_crossings with recorded sessions and independently labeled video."
        )
    ```
  - Furthermore, `IEEE-conference-template-062824/AUDIT_NOTICE.md` confirms:
    *"The legacy research/analysis/generate_paper_figures.py contains literal historical arrays, camera-regime correlations and latency claims that are not established by the corrected simulation evaluation."*
  - The authoritative provenance document (`FINAL_RESULTS_PROVENANCE.md`, Section 2 row 8) states that the published uncertainty calibration artifact (`results/uncertainty_calibration/pilot_20261014/calibration.json`) was fitted exclusively against **synthetic queue-count discrepancies ($\ge 3$ vehicles)** in the custom simulation environment, with only 2 of 7 features active (count volatility and forecast residual).
- **Validation Verdict**: **FABRICATED / UNVERIFIED**. There is no ground-truth human annotation dataset in the repository matching these 1,000 frames. The reported correlation ($r = 0.928$) and per-regime metrics must not be combined with or cited in the primary research evaluation.
- **Reconciliation Resolution**: Excluded from the final manuscript. A genuine real-video uncertainty validation experiment must be conducted using independently annotated datasets (Issue 2).

### 2.2. File 2: `results/final/city_2x2_scalability_v2.json`

- **File Metadata**: 1,763 bytes, JSON format.
- **Associated Tables**: `results/final/table_city_2x2_scalability_v2.csv`, `results/final/table_city_2x2_scalability_v2.tex`.
- **Claimed Content**:
  - Evaluates network-level scalability on a 2x2 grid (intersections A, B, C, D) across 20 frozen seeds (1101–1120) under "Clean" and "Corrupted (30% Miss)" perception.
  - Reported results:
    * Clean Fixed-Time: Mean Delay 12.52 s, 95% CI [10.80, 14.27], P95 Delay 43.67 s, Throughput 1053.0.
    * Clean Max-Pressure: Mean Delay 12.57 s, 95% CI [10.84, 14.33], P95 Delay 43.67 s, Throughput 1050.0.
    * Clean D3QN: Mean Delay 12.52 s, 95% CI [10.80, 14.27], P95 Delay 43.67 s, Throughput 1053.0.
    * Clean FlowSync-UQ: Mean Delay 12.52 s, 95% CI [10.80, 14.27], P95 Delay 43.67 s, Throughput 1053.0.
- **True Provenance & Origin**:
  - Generated by `research/legacy/run_city_scalability_v2.py`.
  - Inspection of the code reveals that:
    1. Fixed-time, D3QN, and FlowSync-UQ yield identical mean delay (12.52 s), identical 95% CIs ([10.80, 14.27] s), identical P95 delay (43.67 s), identical mean queue (9.8), and identical throughput (1053.0).
    2. Under 30% miss corruption, D3QN and FlowSync-UQ again yield the exact same 12.52 s delay and 43.67 s P95 delay.
    3. The script instantiated independent controller instances (`get_controller(name)`) but did not load trained multi-agent policies or coordinate network arterial progression. The simulation vehicle paths and physics in `CityNetwork` resulted in invariant delay measures across these arms.
- **Validation Verdict**: **DEGENERATE / UNRECONCILED**. The reported numbers do not reflect genuine multi-agent RL policy differentiation. The primary pre-registered protocol (`MATCHED_SHIELD_PROTOCOL_20261015.md`) is restricted to an isolated single 4-way intersection.
- **Reconciliation Resolution**: Excluded from primary paper results. Multi-intersection grid scalability is retained as Future Research (Priority 3).

### 2.3. File 3: `results/final/shadow_mode_multi_session.json`

- **File Metadata**: 2,091 bytes, JSON format.
- **Associated Tables**: `results/final/table_shadow_replay_v2.tex`.
- **Claimed Content**:
  - Claims to evaluate candidate controllers on diverse empirical CCTV video arrival traces across three real-world environmental regimes without live infrastructure actuation:
    1. `cctv_session_01_midday` (balanced daylight flow, low occlusion)
    2. `cctv_session_02_rush_commute` (heavy morning peak surge, high queueing, glare)
    3. `cctv_session_03_adverse_rain` (dusk, wet pavement reflections, reduced contrast)
  - Reported results:
    * Session 1 (Midday): Fixed 13.61 s, Greedy 4.97 s, Max-Pressure 4.97 s, D3QN 7.57 s, FlowSync-UQ 20.75 s.
    * Session 2 (Rush): Fixed 14.84 s, Greedy 7.71 s, Max-Pressure 6.06 s, D3QN 10.53 s, FlowSync-UQ 17.00 s.
    * Session 3 (Rain): Fixed 7.96 s, Greedy 7.16 s, Max-Pressure 5.57 s, D3QN 18.69 s, FlowSync-UQ 19.99 s.
- **True Provenance & Origin**:
  - Generated by `research/cv/shadow_mode_multi_session.py`.
  - Inspection of `generate_session_schedule()` in lines 34–70 shows that **no video recordings or empirical vehicle detections were used**:
    ```python
    # Synthetic arrival generation using random numbers:
    platoon_size = int(rng.geometric(p=cluster_rate))
    chosen_idx = int(rng.choice(len(movements), p=weights))
    ```
  - The arrivals were generated entirely from synthetic geometric distributions with hardcoded cluster probabilities (0.55 for rush, 0.35 for rain, 0.40 for midday).
  - Furthermore, under this synthetic schedule, **FlowSync-UQ suffered catastrophic delay degradation** (up to 20.75 s vs. 4.97 s for Max-Pressure in Session 1, and 19.99 s vs. 5.57 s in Session 3), directly contradicting earlier draft claims of fallback superiority.
- **Validation Verdict**: **SYNTHETIC / METHODOLOGICALLY FLAWED**. The data represents an unvalidated synthetic simulation pilot mislabeled as "CCTV video shadow replay."
- **Reconciliation Resolution**: Excluded from the paper. Genuine shadow-mode evaluation requires verified vehicle passage timestamps extracted from recorded video streams.

### 2.4. File 4: `results/final/final_hardened_suite_summary.json`

- **File Metadata**: 8,458 bytes, JSON format.
- **Associated Tables/Reports**: `results/final/table_benchmark_comparison_v2.tex`, `results/final/table_noise_robustness_v2.tex`, `results/final/table_ablations_v2.tex`, `results/final/statistical_report_v2.json`.
- **Claimed Content**:
  - Summarizes an evaluation of Fixed-time, Actuated, Greedy, Max-Pressure, DQN, and D3QN across 20 frozen seeds (1101–1120) under clean conditions and 7 noise profiles.
  - Reported Clean Delay: Fixed 15.61 s, Actuated 15.51 s, Greedy 12.55 s, Max-Pressure 12.73 s, DQN 17.79 s, D3QN 16.50 s.
  - Reported 30% Noise Delay: D3QN 14.94 s, FlowSync-UQ 14.67 s (claimed 1.79% delay reduction, $p = 1.48 \times 10^{-6}$).
- **True Provenance & Origin**:
  - Generated by `research/legacy/run_final_hardened_experiments.py`.
  - This was an early October 2026 experiment that suffered from three fatal methodological limitations:
    1. **Single Policy Replicate**: It evaluated only a single D3QN training run (Seed 42), ignoring between-training-seed variability.
    2. **Premature Training / Overfitting**: The policy used was an unshielded checkpoint evaluated on an ad-hoc noise injector.
    3. **Misleading Statistical Claims**: The claimed $p = 1.48 \times 10^{-6}$ delay reduction for FlowSync-UQ was an artifact of comparing a single training seed against a degraded unshielded baseline. In the rigorous 2,800-run matched shield experiment with 3 training seeds, FlowSync-UQ added +0.015 s delay ($p = 0.702$).
  - This entire experimental setup was formally **superseded on October 15, 2026** by the frozen pre-registered protocol (`MATCHED_SHIELD_PROTOCOL_20261015.md`), which introduced 3 training replicates (Seeds 101, 202, 303), 20 traffic seeds, 14 controller arms, 10 cells, and 5,000-draw crossed-bootstrap inference.
- **Validation Verdict**: **SUPERSEDED**. The contents of this file represent outdated, un-replicated preliminary exploration.
- **Reconciliation Resolution**: Strictly excluded from paper evidence. The authoritative custom simulator results are documented exclusively in `results/replicate_evaluation/matched_shield_v5_20261015/` (2,800 runs).
